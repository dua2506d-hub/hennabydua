The Heart of HennaByDua
A Lifelong Romance with Art

"Henna is a living prayer etched in deep mahogany."

For me, mehndi has never been just wedding makeup. It is an ancient language of blessing, femininity, and celebration passed down through generations.

My journey began over eight years ago. Drawn to the rhythmic repetition of mandalas, the organic grace of botanical vines, and the intoxicating scent of pure cajeput and eucalyptus, I immersed myself in mastering the craft. Over the years, I honed a signature aesthetic that blends royal heritage geometry with modern negative space.

Today, HennaByDua is proud to have adorned over 500 brides across Pakistan, the Middle East, and worldwide destination weddings. Each bridal commission is approached as a bespoke piece of fine art—incorporating intimate milestones, cherished initials, and architectural silhouettes that reflect your unique romance.

Purity & Skin Health
Pure Nature in Every Cone

The 100% Organic Henna Promise

We never compromise on your skin's well-being. Every single batch of henna paste is personally hand-crafted by Dua every week using only 5 certified organic ingredients.

Sojat Henna Powder

Directly sourced from the historic fields of Sojat, Rajasthan. Triple-sifted micro-fine powder ensuring silky, knot-free line work.

Pure Essential Oils

Therapeutic-grade Cajeput, Eucalyptus, and Bulgarian Lavender oils rich in natural terpenes that coax out the darkest stain.

Zero Chemicals or PPD

Strictly zero chemical additives, preservatives, or artificial dyes. Completely safe for expectant mothers and sensitive skin.

Heirloom Rich Stain

Our paste oxidizes over 48 hours to create a majestic, dark mahogany stain that photographs with breathtaking richness.
