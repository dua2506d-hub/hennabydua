Preparation Guide
Your Journey to the Altar

The Bridal Henna Timeline

Follow our proven timeline for a stress-free bridal mehndi experience and the deepest, richest stain possible.

3 to 6 Months Before: Reservation & Consultation

Secure your wedding date with our deposit. We review your bridal outfit photos, jewelry moodboard, and design custom sketch blueprints tailored to your wishes.

1 Month Before: Optional Bridal Henna Trial

Test your skin's affinity with our organic henna, test design elements, and experience the relaxing application process firsthand.

2 to 3 Days Before: Skin Preparation

Complete all waxing, manicures, and pedicures at least 24-48 hours before your henna session. Ensure your skin is free of oils, lotions, and heavy self-tanners on the appointment day.

2 Days Before: The Application Session

Dua arrives at your venue or studio. Wear comfortable clothing with loose sleeves. Relax with music and loved ones as your royal mehndi is applied.

Wedding Day: Deep Mahogany Glow

After 48 hours of natural oxidation, your stain blooms into an intensely dark, breathtaking mahogany that commands the spotlight in all your wedding photos!
