Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book my bridal mehndi?

We recommend reserving your bridal slot 3 to 6 months in advance, especially during the peak wedding season (October to March). A small date reservation deposit secures your booking.

When should bridal mehndi be applied before the wedding day?

Natural henna takes 24 to 48 hours to fully oxidize into its deepest, richest mahogany-maroon stain. Therefore, we always recommend scheduling your bridal mehndi appointment 2 days before your main wedding ceremony.

How do I care for my mehndi to get the darkest stain?

Keep the dried paste on your skin for 6 to 8 hours (or overnight). Avoid water contact for the first 24 hours. Scrape off the paste gently using coconut oil or mustard oil rather than washing with water, and apply our complimentary organic aftercare balm.

Can you incorporate customized portraits, initials, or story elements?

Absolutely! Customized storytelling is Dua's signature forte. You can send us your groom's name, wedding hashtag, love story landmarks, skylines, or proposal motifs during your consultation.

Do you travel to other cities and international destinations?

Yes, Dua regularly travels across Pakistan and internationally for destination weddings in Dubai, Turkey, the UK, Europe, and North America. Travel and lodging arrangements are discussed during the consultation.
